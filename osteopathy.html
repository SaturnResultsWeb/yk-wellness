What osteopathy is

Treating cause, not symptom

Osteopathy is a regulated healthcare discipline that focuses on the musculoskeletal system: the bones, muscles, joints, connective tissues, and how they work together. Our osteopaths are trained to diagnose, treat and manage a wide range of conditions using their hands.

The approach is structural and diagnostic. Rather than applying a standard treatment to a presenting symptom, an osteopath assesses the whole person to understand why a problem has developed, and addresses that root cause.

All YK Wellness osteopaths are registered with the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC), which is the statutory regulatory body for osteopaths in the UK. Registration is legally required to practise.

Conditions treated

What osteopathy can help

  • Back pain and sciatica
  • Neck pain and headaches
  • Joint pain in the hip, knee, shoulder and elbow
  • Postural problems from sitting, standing or overuse
  • Sports injuries and overuse syndromes
  • Recovery following surgery or injury
  • Frozen shoulder and rotator cuff problems
  • Plantar fasciitis and foot and ankle pain
