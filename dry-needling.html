Dry Needling

Precise, fine-needle insertion into trigger points and areas of chronic muscular tension. Often reaches what hands-on techniques cannot, producing immediate and lasting relief.

Book Now ↗ Ask on WhatsApp
What it is

Not acupuncture

Dry needling is a western, evidence-based technique. It uses the same sterile, single-use needles as acupuncture, but the methodology is entirely different. Dry needling targets myofascial trigger points: localised areas of contracted muscle that refer pain and restrict movement.

By inserting a needle precisely into a trigger point, the practitioner creates a local twitch response that causes the contracted muscle fibres to release. This can produce immediate and significant relief in areas that have been resistant to manual therapy alone.

Conditions treated

When to consider dry needling

  • Chronic neck and shoulder tension
  • Lower back pain with a muscular component
  • Trigger points that refer pain to other areas
  • Tennis elbow and golfer's elbow
  • Plantar fasciitis and calf tightness
  • Sports injuries that have plateaued with other treatment
  • Headaches and migraines with a muscular trigger

New to dry needling?

It is normal to feel apprehensive if you have not experienced dry needling before. The needles used are extremely fine — much thinner than injection needles — and most clients describe the sensation as a dull ache or brief twitch rather than pain. We will explain every step before we begin, and you remain in control throughout the session.
