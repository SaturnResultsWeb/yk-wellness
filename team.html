The Practitioners

Your team at YK Wellness

Six practitioners selected for clinical excellence and a commitment to getting real outcomes for their clients. Not a name on a rota. People who care about what happens in the room.

Lead Therapist · Founder

Yury

8+ years of clinical practice in West London. Yury founded YK Wellness on a single principle: that every client deserves a practitioner who actually thinks about their case. He specialises in deep tissue work, sports rehabilitation and complex musculoskeletal presentations.

Osteopath · GOsC Registered

Christos

Fully registered with the General Osteopathic Council. Christos has a particular interest in structural assessment and joint dysfunction, with a rigorous approach to diagnosis that informs every treatment decision. He sees patients with back pain, neck pain, joint problems and postural dysfunction.

Sports Therapist · Dry Needling

Your Therapist

A specialist in sports massage and dry needling with a background working alongside elite athletes. Expert in trigger point therapy, soft tissue release and the management of overuse injuries in active clients.

Massage Therapist · Cupping

Your Therapist

A highly trained massage therapist specialising in Swedish and deep tissue work, with advanced cupping therapy certification. Known for thoroughness and a relaxed but precise approach in the treatment room.

Remedial Therapist

Your Therapist

Specialising in remedial and sports massage with a particular focus on recovery from injury. Works extensively with clients referred from physiotherapy and from medical practitioners for ongoing soft tissue management.

Holistic Therapist

Your Therapist

A holistic practitioner with advanced training in Swedish massage, aromatherapy and relaxation techniques. Works with clients managing stress, chronic fatigue and general physical tension who want a restorative rather than remedial experience.
