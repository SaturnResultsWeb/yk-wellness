Sports Massage

Targeted soft-tissue work designed for active bodies. Whether you are training, competing or recovering, sports massage keeps you moving at your best.

Book Now ↗ Ask on WhatsApp
What it is

Maintenance for an active body

Sports massage is a targeted, technique-driven treatment focused on the soft tissues that take the most load during physical activity. It is not a relaxation massage adapted for athletes. It is a distinct clinical approach.

We work with runners, cyclists, gym-goers, team sport players, and anyone who uses their body hard. The session is structured around your current training phase, recent workload, and any areas that are holding tension or showing early signs of strain.

What it addresses

From performance to recovery

  • Muscle tightness and fatigue between training sessions
  • Reduction of delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS)
  • Prevention of overuse injuries
  • Recovery after competition or heavy training blocks
  • Improved flexibility and range of movement
  • Trigger point release in chronically overloaded muscles
