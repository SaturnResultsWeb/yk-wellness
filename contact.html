Before you book

Common questions

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing. For massage, you will be appropriately draped throughout. We provide towels and gowns if needed.

Can I pay on the day?

Yes. We accept card, contactless and cash. Payment via Fresha at the time of booking is also available.

How do I cancel or rearrange?

Please give us at least 24 hours notice. You can cancel or rebook directly through Fresha or by messaging us on WhatsApp.

I am not sure which treatment I need

Message us on WhatsApp or try our treatment recommender. We will help you choose the right treatment for your situation.

Ready to book?

Book online in two minutes or speak to us first. We are here Monday to Sunday.
