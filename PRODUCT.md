# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in West and Central London, ages 25–55, typically self-referring. Three clusters:

- **Recovery clients** — dealing with chronic back/neck pain, post-injury rehab, or stress-driven tension. Often already know what they need; they're evaluating trust and booking ease.
- **Performance clients** — athletes, runners, gym-goers using Westway Sports Centre or nearby gyms. Want sports massage, dry needling, or osteopathy as part of a training protocol.
- **Referred clients** — sent by a GP, physio, or friend. Lower awareness of what a session involves; need credibility signals to feel confident.

All three are on mobile, often booking on the go. Decision cycle is short — they're comparing 2–3 local options and the site that feels most trustworthy wins.

## Product Purpose

A conversion-first marketing website for YK Wellness, a clinician-led therapeutic practice at Westway Sports Centre, London W10. Purpose: turn a first-time visitor into a booked appointment via Fresha. Secondary goals: rank on London-area search terms (sports massage west london, osteopathy notting hill, deep tissue massage w10), and signal enough clinical credibility that word-of-mouth referrals land confidently.

Success = measurable increase in Fresha bookings, improved local SEO position, and a site that a client is proud to share with a friend.

## Brand Personality

**Precise. Professional. Trusted.**

Voice: Calm, direct, evidence-based — the way a skilled practitioner speaks to a patient: no jargon, no fluff, no overselling. Warm but not soft. Authoritative but never cold.

Emotional goal: The visitor should feel they've found the right place — competent hands, no guesswork. Like the relief of getting a recommendation from someone who actually knows.

## Anti-references

- **Spa / relaxation aesthetic** — candles, lotus, "treat yourself", ambient music language. YK Wellness is clinical-grade, not a day off.
- **Clinical / sterile / boring** — white boxes, NHS-blue, stock photos of anatomical diagrams. Credibility must come through warmth, not coldness.
- **Generic wellness template** — gradient hero with "Your wellness journey starts here", same stock photography used by 200 other clinics, identical 3-card service grid.

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration** — every visual element earns its place. Texture and depth only when they serve hierarchy or warmth; never for style alone.
2. **Credibility before persuasion** — build trust first (credentials, real practitioners, genuine testimonials), then invite the booking. Never the reverse.
3. **Speed on every axis** — fast to load, fast to scan, fast to book. The path from landing to Fresha should require zero thought.
4. **Warmth without softness** — editorial restraint, not clinical coldness. The site should feel like a well-designed private practice, not a holiday spa and not a hospital.
5. **Real over stock** — real practitioner names, real credentials, real address, real Google rating. If it could be on any other clinic's site, it shouldn't be on this one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline:
- Minimum 16px body text, 44px touch targets for older adult users in the user base
- All text contrast at 4.5:1 minimum; large text at 3:1 minimum
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected: all scroll animations and transitions disabled at system level
- Keyboard-navigable booking flow
