Cupping Therapy

An ancient technique applied with clinical precision. Cupping creates suction to lift fascial tissue, improve blood flow and release areas of deep restriction that hands-on massage cannot always reach.

Book Now ↗ Ask on WhatsApp
How it works

Lift, not press

Where massage works by pressing down into tissue, cupping works by creating a vacuum that lifts tissue upward. This negative pressure decompresses the fascia, draws blood flow to the area, and can release restrictions that have developed over time in deeper layers of the soft tissue.

At YK Wellness, cupping is always applied clinically, not cosmetically. It is used as part of a broader treatment plan, often alongside massage or dry needling, where it adds real value to the outcome.

What to expect

Marks are normal

Cupping may leave circular marks on the skin that range from pink to deep red or purple, depending on the level of restriction in the tissue and how long the cups are applied. These marks are not bruises — they are the result of blood being drawn to the surface.

They typically fade within 3 to 7 days. The more restricted the area, the darker the mark tends to be. This is useful diagnostic information as well as a normal part of the treatment.

  • Fascial restriction and tightness
  • Poor local circulation and tissue health
  • Chronic muscle tension that resists hands-on work
  • Recovery enhancement for athletes
  • Areas of localised scar tissue
